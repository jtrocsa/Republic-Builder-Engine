# Architecture Quick Reference

**Read this file first.** Consult the longer documents linked at the bottom only when you need deeper rationale than a bullet here provides. This file must be updated after every architecture or migration phase — see the update rule in `CLAUDE.md`.

## 1. Current project state

- The game is **Chronicle**, an AP U.S. History RPG. "Republic Builder Engine" is retired as the project's identity; the eventual multi-subject platform has no final name yet.
- **Chronicle covers CED Periods 1–7 of 9** as of Phase 89C — twenty-one cases across seven units, all with real, cited historical content. Unit 1 ("The Atlantic World," case-001–003), Unit 2 ("Colonial Crossroads," case-004–006 plus unit review), Unit 3 ("Revolution and Founding," case-007–009), Unit 4 ("Democracy, Markets, and Reform," case-010–012), Unit 5 ("A House Divided," case-013–015), Unit 6 ("A Continent on Paper," case-016–018) and Unit 7 ("The Terms of Belonging," case-019–021). Seven walkable outdoor field maps and **eight** interiors; eighteen generated `.tmj` maps in the repository. **All seven units are at parity as of Phase 89E** — every one has a walkable map, its interiors, and three playable missions on it, twenty-one missions in all. Unit 6 was completed in Phase 88 by its Meridian reveal; Unit 7 by its three activities in Phase 89E (decision log `0081`). Every Chronotravel and every recall opens on a painted plate of its destination as of Phase 88A, reached through a canvas warp and a sync ring as of Phase 88B.
- The entire running game is one file, `apps/web/src/main.js` (16,061 lines as of Phase 90I — re-check with a quick line count before citing it if much time has passed, don't trust a stale figure) — no framework, no React/Vue/Phaser. Two plugin layers sit beside it and hold no APUSH facts: `apps/web/src/quest-types/` (six rubric-scored quest types) and `apps/web/src/engine/activities/` (four playable activity engines, Phase 68). Procedural Web Audio lives in `apps/web/src/engine/audio-engine.js`; real-accounts/classroom/submission/grading logic lives in `apps/web/src/repositories/` (`remote-*`/`progress-repository.js`/`teacher-override-repository.js`) and `apps/web/src/engine/` (`auth-flows.js`, `evaluator-requests.js`, `evaluator-client.js`, `custom-content-authoring.js`).

## 3. Current approved architecture

- Keep working code where it lives (`main.js`, `content/*.js`); wrap it thinly rather than moving it.
- Add `export` to specific pure functions worth unit-testing (collision math, badge logic, save-merge logic) and test them **in place** — no physical extraction of movement/collision/camera/NPC code.
- The long-term domain map (`PlatformCore`, `ContentRegistry`, `WorldComposition`, `QuestEngine`, `WorldRuntime`, `packs/<subject>/`) is real and documented, but is **future direction, not current structure** — see `PLATFORM-ARCHITECTURE-PROPOSAL.md`.

- Phase 61 — the cast's locomotion, scale and grounding: Phase 60 replaced the art but left the systems around it tuned for the placeholders. One ADR: `docs/decision-log/0044-cast-scale-locomotion-and-grounding.md`. **NPCs skated and the player glided.** NPCs ran on `setInterval` with a fixed per-tick displacement and no time scaling — 0.15–0.23 tiles/s against the player's 3.65, a twentieth — while `--sprite-cycle` was declared in the keyframe and set by nothing, so everyone animated at the 0.72s fallback: a 1.30-tile stride for the player, 0.05 for an NPC. `walkCycleSeconds()` now derives the cycle from ground speed via one `STRIDE_TILES` constant (player 0.30s reads as a run, NPC 0.81s as a walk), and NPC movement is time-based at 33ms with cached node lookups. **All 21 NPCs walked the same 0.9×0.65-tile rectangle** from three hand-written waypoint tables; a post is now a home anchor plus a radius and new `engine/npc-wander.js` picks reachable points inside it with varied pauses and idle turns, seeded per NPC id. ## 8. Deferred systems and tools

- **Tools deferred, not rejected:** Phaser 4.1.x, inkjs. Real candidates, no concrete forcing function yet for Phaser (no perf complaint exists yet). **inkjs's stated reason is now out of date** — it read "today's dialogue is one static line per NPC, no branching need exists yet," and `docs/design/MISSION-ACTIVITY-CATALOG.md` §3 is that need arriving on paper. **Still deferred, and Phase 68 strengthened the case rather than weakening it**: the INTERVIEW engine ships branching field dialogue — four questions put to seven speakers, answers that differ by pair — as a **flat content matrix plus a lookup**, in about 200 lines, with the NPC tables untouched.
